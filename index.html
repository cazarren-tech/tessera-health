Privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Who we are

Tessera Health is an Australian health business. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). You can contact us about privacy at info@tesserahealth.com.au.

What we collect

Through this website, we only collect your email address and anything you choose to include when you contact us. The site has no accounts and doesn't save anything you type into it.

How we collect it

When you use "Notify me" or email us directly. The "Notify me" button opens your own email app, so your message comes to us as an ordinary email.

Why we use it

To reply to you and to let you know when we launch. We don't sell or rent your information, and you can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Health information

Health information is "sensitive information" under the Privacy Act. Please don't send health details through this website. Where we handle health information in our services, we'll only collect it with consent and will protect it with extra care.

Storage and security

Emails are stored in our business email system. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we keep it only for as long as we need it for the purposes above.

Overseas disclosure

This site is hosted by Netlify and uses Google Fonts. To deliver the page, these providers process technical details such as your IP address and browser type, and may store them outside Australia, including in the United States. Our email provider may also store data overseas.

Access and correction

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@tesserahealth.com.au.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, please contact us first. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services grow. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Cookies

Last updated 9 October 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser, often to remember settings or track how people use a site.

Cookies we use

None. This site doesn't set any cookies, and there's no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Third-party services

The site is hosted by Netlify and loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Neither sets cookies on this site, but both receive technical details such as your IP address and browser type when the page loads. See Netlify's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy for how they handle that information.

Managing cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies at any time in your browser's settings. Because we don't use cookies, blocking them won't affect this site.

Changes to this notice

If we add analytics or other tools later, we'll update this notice and, where required, ask for your consent first. Questions? Email info@tesserahealth.com.au.
